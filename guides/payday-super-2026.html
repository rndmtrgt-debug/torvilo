Payday Super 2026: how to check your employer contributions

Compare your payslip with your super fund transactions under the rules applying from 1 July 2026.

What changed on 1 July 2026?

Payday Super requires employers to pay super guarantee for each payday rather than relying on the former quarterly payment cycle. The general deadline is for the contribution to reach the fund within seven business days after payday. Some circumstances allow a longer period, including certain new employee situations. Check the ATO's rules before treating a contribution as late.

Calculate the amount you expect

The super guarantee rate is 12% of eligible qualifying earnings. Qualifying earnings generally include ordinary time earnings and certain other payments. Do not simply multiply every payslip dollar by 12% without understanding the earnings categories.

Worked fortnightly example

Illustrative payslip: $2,000 eligible qualifying earnings and $200 separately identified overtime that does not qualify.

Expected minimum contribution for this simplified scenario: $2,000 × 12% = $240. Multiplying the full $2,200 by 12% would produce $264, a different estimate.

Use the take-home pay calculator for a rough super estimate only where the entered earnings qualify.

Reconcile contributions without mixing dates

  1. Record the payday, employer name and the amount shown on the payslip.
  2. Identify which earnings qualify and estimate the contribution.
  3. Open your super fund's transaction history and look for employer contributions, not investment returns or balance movements.
  4. Compare the amounts received and applicable deadlines; ask payroll about combined or split payments and rejected transactions.

When an amount appears missing

First check the fund and employer details. Ask payroll for the payment date, amount and intended fund. Keep the payslip, fund transaction history and response. If the issue remains unresolved, the ATO provides an unpaid-super reporting pathway. Do not email login details or tax file numbers to TORVILO.

Example record to keep

Payday: 10 November; estimated super: $240; amount received: $240; received date: enter the actual date from your fund. If the amount is missing, record the date you checked and the next action instead of assuming a zero contribution is definitive.
