INDUSTRY GUIDE

Contract cleaning pay and work records in Australia

A practical workflow for checking your pay against the applicable employment instrument.

Start with award coverage

The Contract cleaning award (MA000022) is a starting point, not proof that it covers your job. Check its coverage and classifications and whether an enterprise agreement applies.

Records worth keeping

Record each site, travel between sites, broken shifts and minimum engagement periods. Check allowances separately.

Example of a coverage or record issue

A cleaner employed directly by a hotel may not fall under the contract cleaning award.

Reconcile your pay

For illustration only, 8 hours at a verified $30 rate and 2 hours at a verified $45 rate total $330 gross. Compare like-for-like payslip lines, then investigate differences in hours, rates or allowances. These are hypothetical rates, not award entitlements.

Use the mixed-rate calculator and payslip discrepancy tool. Keep evidence using the work records guide.

Official information

Fair Work pay guides and the linked award provide current rates and conditions. Check effective dates and your employment type before calculating.
