Changing Jobs in Australia: A Practical Money Checklist

Check final pay, notice, super, leave, records and cash flow before changing Australian jobs.

Before accepting the new offer

Confirm whether the quoted pay includes super, the expected hours, work location, pay frequency, start date, probation conditions and whether the role is casual, part-time or full-time. Compare the actual terms in writing rather than relying on a job advertisement.

Check notice and handover

Read your current contract and applicable award or enterprise agreement before resigning. Notice requirements can vary. Do not assume you can use annual leave instead of giving notice without agreement.

Understand final pay

Final pay may involve wages owing, unused annual leave where applicable and other entitlements. Personal or sick leave is generally not paid out when employment ends. Rules vary by arrangement and circumstances. Check your final payslip and seek official guidance if figures look wrong.

Keep important records

Download payslips, employment contracts, roster records, timesheets, super fund details and relevant correspondence before losing access to work systems. Do not copy confidential employer information you are not entitled to retain.

Budget for the gap

Estimate the days between your final pay and the first new pay. A fortnightly payroll schedule can create a longer cash-flow gap than expected. List unavoidable bills during that period and keep a small buffer where possible.

Review super and tax

Give the new employer the required tax and super details. Check that contributions arrive in the correct fund. Changing jobs does not by itself determine your final annual tax bill.

Official checks

Use Fair Work's ending employment guidance for notice and final-pay questions, and the ATO for super and tax information. See also TORVILO's job-offer comparison guide.
