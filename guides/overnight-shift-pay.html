How to calculate overnight shift hours and pay

A shift that crosses midnight can be confusing to reconcile. Record the actual dates, paid breaks and rate periods before estimating gross earnings.

Calculate time across midnight

For an ordinary clock night, a shift beginning at 10 pm and finishing at 5:30 am has two hours before midnight and five and a half afterwards: 7.5 hours elapsed. Subtract a 30-minute unpaid break to obtain seven paid hours. Record paid breaks as paid time, not as deductions.

Single-rate scenario

7 paid hours × a verified $30 hourly rate × an illustrative 1.25 multiplier = $262.50 gross.

Split rate periods instead of averaging them

Suppose your award or agreement specifies an example rate of $30 before midnight and $40 afterwards. A 30-minute unpaid break takes place after midnight. Under those hypothetical rules, the first two hours yield $60 and the next five paid hours yield $200: $260 total gross. If the break instead occurred before midnight, the amounts would be $45 and $220, or $265 total. The break's location matters when rates differ.

These rates are illustrations, not statutory entitlements. Some instruments classify a shift by its start or finish time, apply a fixed amount, or define a shift differently from a calendar-day split. Check the applicable instrument before splitting the pay.

Weekend, public holiday and overtime boundaries

Keep separate records of shift dates, actual start and finish times, breaks, ordinary hours, additional hours and allowances. Check the relevant rules for a shift spanning a weekend or public holiday and whether overtime and other loadings interact. Never assume two multipliers must be added together.

Daylight saving and pay-period cut-offs

On a clock-change night, elapsed time may differ from wall-clock subtraction. Fair Work provides guidance on overnight daylight saving shifts, including the treatment where an award or agreement is silent. The TORVILO overnight calculator assumes a normal clock night and a shift shorter than 24 hours; verify daylight saving separately.

When a shift crosses the payroll period boundary, check which payslip records the hours rather than counting it twice. Record both dates in your own timesheet.

Keep evidence that is useful at payday

For each shift, record start and finish dates, break times, any higher-rate intervals and the source of your verified rates. Save the calculation alongside your roster. Use the free shift and overtime Excel tracker for your own records.
