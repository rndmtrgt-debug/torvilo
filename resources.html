How to use these templates

Download a CSV, open it in your spreadsheet program and replace the example rows with your own information. CSV files are plain data: they do not contain automatic formulas, charts, password protection or macros. For calculations, use TORVILO's free calculators or create spreadsheet formulas yourself.

Privacy and backups

The files download to your device. TORVILO does not receive information you type into your local copy. Save a backup in a location you control and take care when sharing files containing personal income or employment details.

Understand the limits

These are organisational worksheets, not tax records certified by the ATO, legal advice or a replacement for payslips. Keep original supporting documents and verify employment entitlements with Fair Work and tax information with the ATO.
