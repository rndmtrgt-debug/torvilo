Employment and payslip calculators

Six practical tools for comparing work and pay. Enter verified rates and entitlements; results are estimates, not legal or tax advice.

Payslip discrepancy

Compare the same pay period and gross amounts before deductions. A difference is not proof of underpayment.

Annual leave estimate

Use your verified entitlement. Exclude periods that do not accrue leave. Four weeks is an illustrative input; eligible shiftworkers may have different entitlements. Read the annual leave accrual guide or download the Excel tracker.

Overnight shift pay

Enter 5.5 for 5:30 am. Assumes one shift under 24 hours, a normal clock night and one rate; split mixed rates using the overtime tool. See worked overnight shift examples.

Casual versus permanent

Include paid leave in paid weeks; compare other entitlements separately.

Job offer comparison

Salary less commuting cost is not take-home pay. Compare super, leave and tax separately.

Pay rise

Gross figures before tax and super.

Check your entitlements with Fair Work. See also mixed-rate overtime, take-home pay and work records.
