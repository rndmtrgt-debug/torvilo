What to record for each shift

Add the date, paid hours, hourly rate and any tips you want included. TORVILO calculates a total for each row and a combined summary, giving you a simple personal record to compare with other work information.

Where your shift records are stored

Your tracker entries are saved in local browser storage on the device and browser you are using. They are not an online account or cloud backup, so clearing browser data, changing devices or using another browser can make those locally stored records unavailable.

Export a backup

Use Export CSV to download a copy of the tracker records. Keeping periodic exports can give you a separate record that can be opened in spreadsheet software.

Useful next steps

Use the Shift Pay Calculator to estimate a shift before recording it, or the Paid Hours & Break Calculator to work out paid hours. When comparing your records with a payment, see How to check your pay and Understanding Australian pay rates.

Why keep independent records?

Rosters and payroll systems are useful, but keeping your own record gives you something independent to compare with them. If a shift is edited later or a pay-period total looks unfamiliar, dates and paid hours recorded close to the time can help you identify the specific difference.

A simple weekly routine

After each shift, record the date, paid hours, rate and tips you want to track. At the end of the week, scan the entries for missing shifts and export a CSV if you want a separate backup. When the payslip arrives, compare the same pay-period dates rather than comparing unrelated weekly totals.

What the tracker total means

The row total is based on the hours and hourly rate you enter, plus the recorded tip amount. It is a personal arithmetic record, not a payroll calculation: it does not automatically apply penalty rates, allowances, tax or super. If a shift contains different rates, calculate those components separately before using a simplified tracker figure.

Privacy and device changes

Local storage means TORVILO does not need an account to hold these entries, but it also means TORVILO cannot restore them if browser data is erased. Before changing phones, browsers or clearing site data, export records you want to keep.
