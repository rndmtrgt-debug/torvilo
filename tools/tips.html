How the weekly tips calculator works

Enter the tips received for each day of the week. TORVILO adds the seven daily amounts to show a weekly total and calculates an average across all seven calendar days.

Keep your own tip records

Recording tips by date can make weekly totals easier to check later. Days with no tips can be left at zero so the weekly record still covers the full seven-day period.

Useful next steps

If you want to record tips alongside paid hours and hourly rates for individual shifts, use the Shift Tracker. To estimate wages separately, use the Shift Pay Calculator.

About the average

The displayed average is the weekly tip total divided by seven calendar days. It is not an average per shift or per day worked unless you worked all seven days.

Average per day versus average per shift

This calculator deliberately divides by seven calendar days. If you received $140 in tips across a week, the displayed calendar-day average is $20 even if you only worked four shifts. For an average per shift, divide your weekly total by the number of shifts you actually worked.

Cash and electronic tips

If you want a complete personal record, record tips consistently regardless of how they were received. A platform, card terminal or workplace may report electronic tips separately from cash tips, so keeping dates with your own totals can make later reconciliation easier.

Do not mix tips with base wages

Tips can be useful when looking at total money received, but they should not obscure the hourly wage or other employment payments you are checking. Keep wage calculations and tip records identifiable so you can compare your wage with the applicable employment information.

Build a better record

The Shift Tracker lets you record tips alongside individual shifts, paid hours and hourly rates. Exporting those entries periodically gives you a separate CSV copy rather than relying only on a weekly total.
