How the delivery profit calculator works

Enter your delivery income, fuel cost, other session costs, hours online and kilometres driven. TORVILO subtracts the entered costs from income and shows estimated operating profit, profit per hour and profit per kilometre.

Why track more than delivery income?

Gross platform income does not show what remains after operating costs. Recording both income and costs can give you a clearer picture of how an individual delivery session performed.

What can count as other costs?

The other-costs field can be used for session costs you want included in your own profitability calculation. It is not a tax-deduction calculator, and an expense being included here does not mean it is deductible for tax purposes.

Useful next steps

Profit is different from cash received

A platform payout tells you revenue for the session, not necessarily what the work earned after costs. Subtracting fuel and other costs creates a simple operating-profit estimate. Dividing that estimate by hours and kilometres can make sessions of different lengths easier to compare.

Example session

If delivery income is $120, fuel is $22 and other entered costs are $8, the simple operating-profit estimate is $90. Over 4 hours that is $22.50 per hour, and over 80 kilometres it is $1.125 per kilometre. Those figures are profitability measures, not taxable-income or tax-deduction calculations.

Costs that are easy to overlook

Fuel is visible because it is purchased regularly, but delivery work can also involve vehicle servicing, tyres, depreciation, insurance, registration, phone/data costs and equipment. Some are not easily allocated to a single session. You can use the other-cost field for your own session comparison, but avoid treating the result as a tax claim.

Compare sessions consistently

Choose a consistent method for recording when a session starts and ends, kilometres driven and which costs you allocate. Consistency makes your own week-to-week comparisons more meaningful even when platform demand and fuel prices change.

Keep the evidence behind the number

A calculator result is not a record by itself. Keep platform statements and relevant receipts or other supporting records. See Gig work income and expense records for a practical record-keeping checklist.
