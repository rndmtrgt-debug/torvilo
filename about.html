About TORVILO

TORVILO is an independent set of free browser-based tools designed to make everyday work, shift and gig-work calculations easier.

What TORVILO does

The focus is practical tools: pay estimates, shift records, tips, delivery-profit calculations and plain-English worker guides for people working in Australia.

How information is prepared

TORVILO aims to keep factual workplace and tax-related context tied to primary Australian sources such as the Fair Work Ombudsman and Australian Taxation Office. Where a rate, rule or requirement can change, users are encouraged to verify current official information for the period and circumstances they are checking.

Calculator methodology

TORVILO calculators perform arithmetic using the figures you enter. They do not automatically identify your award, agreement, classification, overtime rules, allowances, tax position or other circumstances that may affect a real entitlement or financial outcome.

Independence

TORVILO is not affiliated with the Fair Work Ombudsman, Fair Work Commission, Australian Taxation Office, an employer, payroll provider or delivery platform.

Corrections and feedback

If you notice information that may be outdated or incorrect, see the Contact TORVILO page. Please do not send sensitive personal, payroll, tax or identity information.

Editorial approach

TORVILO is built around a simple rule: explain the calculation, state the assumptions, identify what the tool cannot determine, and link to an authoritative source when a workplace or tax rule matters. Guides are written for TORVILO rather than copied from official material; official sources are used to verify factual context and give readers somewhere authoritative to continue.

Updates and review

Time-sensitive figures are reviewed when new Australian rates or rules take effect. Calculator pages identify the period their tax assumptions relate to. If a guide contains a current rate, readers should still verify the official source for the date they are checking.

What TORVILO is not

TORVILO is not a payroll service, tax agent, financial adviser or workplace-law service. It does not collect enough information to determine an individual legal entitlement or tax outcome. Its purpose is to make arithmetic, record keeping and the process of checking official information easier.
